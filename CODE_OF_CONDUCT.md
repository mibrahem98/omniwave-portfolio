# OmniWave Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OmniWave is a local-first music player built for deliberate listening. We want its contributor space to be constructive, technically rigorous, privacy-aware, and welcoming to people with different identities, languages, devices, and levels of experience. Everyone who participates in this repository is expected to help maintain that standard.

## Expected conduct

Contributors communicate with respect, focus discussion on the work, and give feedback that is specific and actionable. We welcome thoughtful disagreement about product decisions, implementation details, accessibility, localization, privacy, and testing when it is expressed professionally.

The project protects people as well as their data. Please remove personal information, audio files, local paths, audio URIs, tokens, credentials, listening history, and other sensitive content from issues, pull requests, screenshots, recordings, and review comments. Treat Arabic RTL behavior, localized copy, reduced-motion support, and accessible controls as first-class product requirements rather than optional polish.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, bad-faith misrepresentation, or publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. So are attempts to bypass the project’s local-first privacy boundaries, to introduce hidden analytics or data transfer, or to share user files and device-specific data without clear authorization.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-owned documentation, and any other interaction representing OmniWave. It also applies when someone is acting as a project representative in public spaces.

## Reporting and response

If you experience or observe conduct that conflicts with this document, use GitHub’s repository-reporting controls where available or contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Include only the information needed to assess the concern and do not reproduce sensitive data unnecessarily.

Reports are handled as privately and impartially as practical. The maintainer may request clarification, remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or take other proportional action to protect participants and the project. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Contribution standard

By participating, you agree to follow this code together with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Questions about a proposed contribution should be raised early so that privacy, accessibility, localization, and native-device validation expectations are clear before implementation.
